Before you launch another campaign you need one number: how many more sends a day the mailboxes can take. `senderCapacity` gives the two sides of it: the daily limits of your active senders added up, and the daily limits of your active campaigns added up, with what was really sent over the last days. `warmupReadiness` lists the senders that are not ready to send, and `senderLoad` lists senders spread over too many campaigns, senders attached to none, and campaigns resting on one sender.

The subtraction, and the decision, are yours. The SDK returns the counts and says which ones the vendor did not report. It moves no sender and changes no limit.

## Before you start

- Node 22 or later.
- The code below runs on the in-memory adapter, so it needs no vendor key. Each sample on this page is a file in the repository that the test suite runs; the output shown is what it printed.
- For real data: an Instantly, Smartlead or HeyReach API key. A read-only key is enough.
- The number of sends a day the new campaign should make.

What the numbers mean:

- **Capacity** is the sum of `dailyLimit` over senders whose status is `active`. A sender with no reported limit is counted in `capacity.withoutLimit`, never as 0. Capacity includes active senders that are not ready, which is why the function lists them underneath.
- **The ask** is the sum of the daily limits of active campaigns, read from each campaign's settings. A campaign with no reported limit is counted in `ask.withoutLimit`. `askRatio` over 1 means the campaigns already ask for more than the senders can send.
- **`sends.averageDaily`** is messages sent over the last `days` complete days (default 7, ending yesterday) divided by `days`. A day the vendor has no row for counts as no sends. `utilization` is that average divided by capacity.
- **`shortCampaigns`** are campaigns whose own active senders cannot cover the campaign's daily limit. A sender shared with another campaign is counted in full for each, so this check is generous too.
- **Not ready** (`warmupReadiness`) means any of: status not active, warmup off, warmup score under `min_score` (default 90) or not reported, or no daily limit. A missing score counts as not ready. A LinkedIn seat is judged on its status only. Pass `check_health: true` to also check DNS records and warmup spam, at one more read per mailbox.
- **Overused** (`senderLoad`) means attached to more than `max_campaigns_per_sender` campaigns (default 3). Only campaigns in `statuses` are counted (default `["active"]`), so a sender used only by a paused campaign shows as unused.

In the output above, headroom is 120 minus 110. But one of the four senders behind the 120 has a warmup score of 72, and one campaign is already short on its own senders. The honest answer to "can we add 40 a day" is no, and the unused mailbox at the bottom is where to start.

## What it costs in vendor requests

- `senderCapacity`: one listing of senders (up to `max_senders`, default 200), one listing of active campaigns (up to `max_campaigns`, default 25), then for each campaign one read of its settings and one listing of its senders, and one read of daily stats. When the adapter has no workspace stats, daily stats cost one read per campaign instead.
- `warmupReadiness`: one listing of senders (up to `max_senders`, default 100). With `check_health`, one health read per mailbox more.
- `senderLoad`: one listing of senders, one listing of campaigns per status, then one listing of senders per campaign.

The function above reads the senders three times and each campaign's senders twice, because each insight makes its own reads. If that matters, put the [cache plugin](/docs/plugins/cache) on the instance. On Instantly, listing one campaign's senders reads every mailbox in the workspace at two requests per page, so the per-campaign part is the expensive one.

| What happened | What you get | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No active sender reports a daily limit | `capacity.daily` is `null` and the finding says capacity is unknown | Read the limits in the vendor tool. Do not treat it as 0. |
| The adapter cannot read campaign settings | `ask` is `null`, with a note | State capacity and recent sends only, as the function does. |
| More active campaigns than `max_campaigns` | `meta.complete` is `false`; the ask covers the campaigns read, so it is too low | Raise the cap (up to 200). |
| Input out of range | `Invalid`, before any request | Fix the value. |
| The adapter cannot list senders | `Unsupported`, before any request | None of the three insights can run. |
| The adapter cannot list a campaign's senders | `senderLoad` is `Unsupported`; `senderCapacity` returns `shortCampaigns` `null` with a note | Use `senderCapacity` and `warmupReadiness` alone. |

## Limits

- Headroom is limits minus limits. It says nothing about whether a vendor or a mailbox provider will let that volume through.
- Capacity counts every active sender in full, ready or not, and mixes mailboxes and LinkedIn seats in one total. `capacity.mailboxDaily` and `capacity.linkedinDaily` split it.
- A campaign whose limit or whose sender's limit is not reported is left out of `shortCampaigns` and counted in `uncheckedCampaigns`.
- `senderCapacity` looks at `active` campaigns only. A paused campaign you plan to resume is not in the ask.
- When the average daily sends come from the campaigns read and not the workspace, a note says so and the average is a floor.
