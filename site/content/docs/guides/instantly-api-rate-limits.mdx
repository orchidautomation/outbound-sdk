**Instantly's API V2 allows 100 requests a second and 6,000 a minute per workspace, and either limit blocks a request. Listing emails, which is how replies are read (`GET /api/v2/emails`), has its own limit of 20 requests a minute, so at 100 emails a page you can read at most 2,000 emails a minute. Over a limit Instantly answers `429` with no `Retry-After` header.** outbound-sdk declares all of these, keeps each process to half of them by default, and waits out a 429 for you, so you write no sleep or retry loop.

## The limits, from Instantly's documentation

From Instantly's [API V2 reference](https://developer.instantly.ai/), fetched 2026-11-14 and recorded in the repository's research note:

| Limit | Applies to |
| --- | --- |
| 100 requests a second | every endpoint, per workspace |
| 6,000 requests a minute | every endpoint, per workspace |
| 20 requests a minute | `GET /api/v2/emails`: listing emails and replies |
| 500 requests per 30 days | `POST /lead-labels/ai-reply-label` |
| 100 calls a day, 200 pages a day | the two local business search endpoints |

- **No rate-limit headers** are documented, and no `Retry-After` comes with a 429 on the general API.
- **The emails limit's scope is not stated.** Two processes reading one workspace slowed each other down in a test on 2026-11-16, so the SDK treats it as shared by every process and key on the workspace. That is inferred, not documented.
- **A page holds at most 100 records** on the lists the SDK reads.

## What was measured

On one live workspace, read-only:

- **2026-11-14:** 300 reads fired at once all succeeded with no 429: 1,505 requests in 30 seconds.
- **2026-11-20:** about 10 email listings a minute got through from one process, half the documented 20. That is the SDK's default share; another reader of the same workspace could not be ruled out.

One workspace, so treat these as one data point. See [what is verified](/docs/status).

The `emails` lane is the 20-a-minute limit at the default share of one half. What that means in practice:

- **Replies are slow to read in bulk.** About 1,000 emails a minute at the default share. An insight that reads conversations, such as [unanswered replies](/docs/guides/unanswered-replies), gets about ten email reads inside the MCP server's 50-second budget, and reads the longest-waiting first.
- **Several processes on one key** each keep their own budget unless they share a store. Two workers at half each together reach Instantly's full limit and draw 429s. [Share one budget with Redis](/docs/guides/shared-rate-budget).
- **On a 429** the lane pauses for one slot of its window (3 seconds on 20 a minute, since there is no `Retry-After`), the rate halves, and the request is retried. See [rate limits](/docs/rate-limits) for the full rule and [retries](/docs/retries) for the bounds.
- **To use more of the limit,** set `share` on `createOutbound` (`1` is the whole published limit). Only do that when nothing else reads the workspace.
