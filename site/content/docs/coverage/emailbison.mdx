Inventoried on 2026-11-15 from EmailBison's API reference at `https://dedi.emailbison.com/api/reference`, read through the OpenAPI document behind it (`https://dedi.emailbison.com/api/reference.openapi`, 180 operations). The table has 180 endpoints: 28 normalized, 32 native, 5 excluded and 115 writes. Paths are relative to your instance, the URL you log in to EmailBison with.

Notes on the count:

- Nothing here was checked against a live account. The adapter is `unverified`.
- `POST /api/campaigns/{campaign_id}/stats` only reads, so it counts as a read.
- Two reads stay in the native catalog and are also used by a normalized read: `messages.list` reads `GET /api/scheduled-emails` (sent emails only), and `stats.byDay` and `stats.bySender` read `GET /api/campaign-events/stats` for one sender.
- The workspace reads are excluded because each workspace carries its webhook signing secret. `workspaces` still lists them through `GET /api/workspaces/v1.1`, and drops the secret from every record.
