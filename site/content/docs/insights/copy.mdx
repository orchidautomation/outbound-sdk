Six insights about what a campaign says and when. Each is a method on `outbound.insights` once the [insights plugin](/docs/insights) is added. What every answer carries and how inputs are checked is on the [overview](/docs/insights).

Three of them need per-step stats (`stats.byStep`). An adapter that does not declare it refuses them as `Unsupported`: see [which adapter serves which](/docs/insights#which-adapter-serves-which).

A reply rate here is replies divided by leads contacted, or by messages sent when the vendor does not count leads. A rate is `count`, `of`, `rate`, `low` and `high`, with `basis` saying what `of` counts. `campaign` in every answer is the campaign's ref, even when the input was its bare vendor id.

## `stepFunnel`

Where a campaign's replies come from, one row per sequence step.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `campaign` | required | a campaign ref | The campaign to read. |
| `from` | `null` | `YYYY-MM-DD` or `null` | First day counted. `null` for the whole history. |
| `to` | `null` | `YYYY-MM-DD` or `null` | Last day counted. `null` for up to today. |
| `min_reply_rate` | 0 | 0 to 1 | A step earns its place when the low end of its reply rate interval is above this. 0 asks only that the rate is reliably above zero. |

`data` (`StepFunnel`):

- `finding`: what the numbers say, in one or two sentences.
- `topStep`: the step with the most replies, or `null`.
- `lastStepEarningPlace`: the last step whose reply rate is reliably above `min_reply_rate`, or `null`.
- `steps`: one entry per step, counted from 0 (a sentence or card counts from 1, as a person does), with `action`, `delayDays`, `inSequence`, `sent`, `replies`, `rates.replyRate` (`count`, `of`, `rate`, `low`, `high` and `basis`), `shareOfReplies`, `sentDrop` and `sentDropRate` (how far sends fell from the step before), `earnsPlace`, and `variants` (empty when the vendor does not split step stats by variant).
- `totals`: `sent` and `replies`.
- `unattributed`: counts the vendor tied to no step (`rows`, `sent`, `replies`). They are in no step's row.

- **Needs:** `sequence`, `stats.byStep`.
- **Costs:** 2 requests.
- **Notes it can add:** some counts are tied to no step by the vendor and are reported as unattributed.
- **Does not:** tell you why a step is weak. A step whose stats name it but which the sequence no longer has is listed with `inSequence: false`. A `null` count means the vendor does not report it.

## `variantTest`

Whether a step's A/B leader is ahead by more than chance.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `campaign` | required | a campaign ref | The campaign to read. |
| `metric` | `replied` | `replied`, `positive`, `opened`, `clicked` | What a variant is judged on. Replies and positive replies are divided by leads contacted (or sends); opens and clicks by messages delivered (or sends). |
| `alpha` | 0.05 | above 0, up to 0.5 | A difference with a p-value under this is a winner. |
| `min_sent` | 100 | 1 or more, whole | A variant with a smaller base than this is not compared. |
| `from` | `null` | `YYYY-MM-DD` or `null` | First day counted. `null` for the whole history. |
| `to` | `null` | `YYYY-MM-DD` or `null` | Last day counted. `null` for up to today. |

`data` (`VariantTest`):

- `campaign`, `metric`, `alpha`: what was asked.
- `variantStatsReported`: `false` when the vendor's step stats carry no variant rows at all, and `null` when it reported no step stats in the date range, which says nothing either way.
- `steps`: one entry per step with two or more variants, with `verdict` (`winner`, `no_difference_yet` or `too_little_data`), `leader`, `pValue` (the leader against its closest rival), `moreSendsNeeded`, a `reason` sentence, and `variants` (each with `sent`, `count`, `of`, `basis`, `rate`, `low`, `high`, `enoughData` and `pValueVsLeader`). Winners first.
- Every variant of a step is divided by the same base, named in `basis`: leads contacted when they all count them, otherwise messages sent (for opens and clicks, messages delivered, otherwise sent). Variants with no base in common are `too_little_data`, with the reason and each variant's own `basis`.
- `totals`: `stepsTested`, `winners`, `noDifferenceYet` and `tooLittleData`.

- **Needs:** `stats.byStep`.
- **Costs:** 1 request.
- **Notes it can add:** the vendor reported no step stats in the date range, so there is nothing to compare; the step stats carry no variant rows, so there is nothing to compare.
- **Does not:** correct for comparing three or more variants. A leader must beat every other variant with enough data to be called a winner. `moreSendsNeeded` is rough: it assumes both rates stay as they are. It pauses nothing.

## `copyAudit`

Problems in a campaign's copy, found from fixed rules before it sends.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `campaign` | required | a campaign ref | The campaign to audit. |
| `max_subject_chars` | 60 | 0 or more, whole | The most characters a subject may have. |
| `max_body_words` | 150 | 0 or more, whole | The most words a body may have. |
| `max_links` | 1 | 0 or more, whole | The most links an email body may have. |
| `max_caps_words` | 1 | 0 or more, whole | How many words of four or more letters may be in capitals, so one acronym is not a finding. |
| `max_exclamations` | 1 | 0 or more, whole | How many exclamation marks a message may have. |
| `max_connection_chars` | 300 | 0 or more, whole | The most characters a LinkedIn connection request may have. |
| `spam_words` | 14 phrases | at least 1 character each | Words and phrases to flag in email copy, matched whole and without case. Replaces the default list. |

The default `spam_words` are: 100% free, act now, buy now, cash bonus, click here, double your, earn money, free trial, guarantee, limited time, no obligation, risk-free, urgent, winner.

`data` (`CopyAudit`):

- `campaign`, `campaignName` (from the campaign record it reads), `channel`.
- `clean`: `true` when no check found anything in the copy that could be read.
- `findings`: one entry per problem, with `step`, `variantKey`, `action`, `check`, `severity`, a `finding` sentence and the `text` that triggered it. High severity first.
- `byCheck`: every check that was run, with how often it fired.
- `bySeverity`: `high`, `medium`, `low`.
- `stepsChecked`, `variantsChecked`, `variantsWithoutBody`.

The checks:

| Check | Severity | Fires when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `malformed_variable` | high | Braces are unbalanced, or a variable has no name. |
| `missing_subject` | high | The first email has no subject. Email only. |
| `connection_request_too_long` | high | A connection request is over the limit. LinkedIn only. |
| `spam_words` | medium | The copy uses a listed word or phrase. Email only. |
| `too_many_links` | medium | The body has more links than allowed. A link written as `<a href="…">` counts once, whatever its text; a bare address outside one counts too; an image's address does not. Email only. |
| `image` | medium | The body holds an image or a tracking pixel. Email only. |
| `subject_breaks_thread` | medium | A follow-up's subject differs from the first email's, so it starts a new thread. Email only. |
| `subject_too_long` | low | Email only. |
| `body_too_long` | low |  |
| `all_caps` | low |  |
| `exclamation_marks` | low |  |

- **Needs:** `sequence`.
- **Costs:** 2 requests.
- **Notes it can add:** some variants have no body in the vendor's answer, so their body checks were skipped.
- **Does not:** read the copy with a model, judge tone or check facts. It reads template text, never a rendered message. A variant whose body the vendor does not return is counted in `variantsWithoutBody`, not judged.

## `copyPreview`

A campaign's copy as written and as one real lead receives it. Use it to read copy that is all or partly templated, such as an Instantly campaign whose subject is `{{subject1}}` and whose body is `{{content1}}`, filled from each lead's custom fields.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `campaign` | required | a campaign ref | The campaign to preview. |
| `lead` | `null` | a lead ref | The lead to render the copy for. `null` picks the lead read with the most of the copy's variables filled. |
| `max_leads` | 100 | 1 to 1,000 | The most leads to read when picking one. Ignored when `lead` is given. |

Variables are found and filled the way [`personalizationGaps`](/docs/insights/people) reads them: `{{name}}`, `{{name|fallback}}`, `{name}` and `%name%`, matched to first name, last name, company, title, email, or a custom field of the same name without regard to case, spaces, underscores or hyphens. A lead with no value gets the copy's fallback; with no fallback the variable is left as written.

`data` (`CopyPreview`):

- `campaign`.
- `lead`: the lead's `lead` ref, `email`, `linkedinUrl` and `variablesFilled`, or `null` when no lead was read.
- `steps`: each step's `step`, `action`, `delayDays` and `variants`. A variant holds `variantKey`, `template` (`subject` and `body` exactly as stored), `rendered` (`subject`, `body` and `bodyText`, the rendered body as plain text) and `unfilled`, the variables left as written.
- `totals`: `steps`, `variants`, `variables` and `unfilled`.

- **Needs:** `sequence`, `leads.byCampaign`.
- **Costs:** 1 request for the sequence, plus 1 for a given lead or one per page of leads read. No lead is read when the copy has no variables.
- **Notes it can add:** the copy uses no variables, so no lead was read; the campaign has no leads, so the copy is shown as written; the redact plugin masks the lead's fields, so the rendered copy shows them masked (`outbound mcp` redacts by default; start it with `--no-redact` to see the values).
- **Does not:** fill a sender signature or another variable the sending tool fills itself at send time, resolve spintax such as `{Hi|Hello}`, or show what was actually sent. It renders the stored template; a vendor may format the sent message differently.

## `copyReuse`

How the same piece of copy performs in different campaigns. Variants are matched by the hash of their subject and body.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `statuses` | `["active", "paused", "completed"]` | at least one campaign status | The campaign statuses to read. |
| `max_campaigns` | 20 | 1 to 200 | The most campaigns to read. |
| `min_sent` | 100 | 1 or more, whole | The smallest base a campaign needs to count in the spread, and a piece of copy needs to be named the best. |

`data` (`CopyReuse`):

- `basis`: what every reply rate is divided by: `contacted` (leads contacted) when every campaign counts them, otherwise `sent` (messages sent). `null` when the campaigns report no base in common; their rates are then listed with their own `basis` but not compared, so every `spread` and `bestCopy` is `null`.
- `reused`: every piece of copy used in two or more campaigns, with `contentHash`, `subject`, `bodyPreview` (the first 120 characters), `campaigns`, pooled `sent`, `replies`, `basis` and `rates.replyRate`, `uses` (per campaign: `campaign`, `campaignName`, `places` as `step:variant`, counts, `basis` and `rates.replyRate`), `spread` (best minus worst reply rate), `best`, `worst`, `spreadPValue`, `spreadIsReal` and a `finding` sentence. Widest spread first.
- `bestCopy`: the copy with the highest pooled reply rate among those with a base of `min_sent`, or `null`.
- `campaignsRead`, `moreCampaignsExist` (`true` when `max_campaigns` stopped the listing with campaigns left), `piecesOfCopy`, `variantsWithoutStats`.

- **Needs:** `sequence`, `stats.byStep`.
- **Costs:** 2 requests per campaign, plus the campaign listing.
- **Notes it can add:** some variants have no counts of their own, because the vendor reports their step as a whole or not at all, so their copy is listed without numbers; the campaigns report different bases, naming each one's, so no spread is judged and no best copy is named; a cap was reached.
- **Does not:** match copy that differs by one character: the hash is of the exact subject and body. The spread test is always at the 0.05 bar. When a step has several variants and the vendor does not split them, those variants have no numbers.

## `replyTiming`

How fast people reply, and on which days and hours.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `campaign` | `null` | a campaign ref | Look at one campaign. An unknown campaign is `NotFound`. `null` looks at every campaign. |
| `since` | `null` | ISO 8601 date and time with offset | Only messages from this time on. `null` means 30 days before now. |
| `max_messages` | 2,000 | 1 to 50,000 | The most messages to read, sent and received together. |
| `timezone_offset_minutes` | 0 | -720 to 840, whole | Minutes to add to UTC for the weekday and hour tables, for example -300 for New York in winter. |

`data` (`ReplyTiming`):

- `campaign`, `since`, `timezoneOffsetMinutes`: what was used.
- `timeToReply`: `sample` (replies paired with the message they answer), `medianHours`, `p75Hours`, `p90Hours`, and the share `within1Hour`, `within24Hours` and `within72Hours`.
- `replies`: when replies arrive, as `byWeekday` (Monday first), `byHour` (0 to 23), `topWeekday` and `topHour`.
- `sendsThatDrewReplies`: the same tables for the sends that drew a reply.
- `counts`: `messagesRead`, `sent`, `received`, `paired`, `followOnReplies`, `unpaired` and `unpairedShare` (`unpaired` over `received`, `null` with no reply).

Sends are read from the same `since` as replies. A reply to a send from before `since` cannot pair, and those are the slow replies, so time to reply leaves them out and can look faster than it is. When `unpairedShare` is over 0.25, `meta.complete` is `false` and a note gives the count. Set `since` earlier to pair more.

- **Needs:** `messages.list`.
- **Costs:** 1 request per page of messages, up to `max_messages`.
- **Notes it can add:** over a quarter of the replies were not paired with a send, with the count; automatic replies such as out-of-office messages are counted as replies, since messages carry no auto flag (on every answer with a reply); the adapter cannot filter messages by date, so the cap applies to all messages and older ones were dropped after reading; a cap was reached.
- **Does not:** separate automatic replies, because messages carry no automatic flag. A reply is paired with the last message sent before it in the same thread, or to the same lead. A further message from someone who already replied is counted in `followOnReplies` and not timed. A reply with no earlier send in what was read is `unpaired`. The offset is fixed, so it does not follow daylight saving.
