Six insights about the mailboxes and LinkedIn seats that send, and the settings campaigns send under. Each is a method on `outbound.insights` once the [insights plugin](/docs/insights) is added. What every answer carries and how inputs are checked is on the [overview](/docs/insights).

These insights report. None of them pauses a sender, changes a limit or edits a setting.

## The data the samples run on

One tool with five mailboxes on three sending domains, one LinkedIn seat and four active campaigns.

## `domainHealth`

Deliverability per sending domain: DNS records, sign-in, block lists and the share of warmup emails landing in spam, with a risk level and the reasons.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `max_senders` | 50 | 1 to 500 | The most senders to check. |
| `spam_rate_threshold` | 0.1 | 0 to 1 | A domain whose warmup emails land in spam more often than this share is high risk. |

`data` (`DomainHealth`):

- `domains`: one entry per domain, worst first, with `risk` (`high`, `medium`, `low` or `unknown`), `reasons`, `mailboxes` (each with `address`, `status`, `connected`, `blacklisted`, `failingDns` and the vendor's own `issues`), `failingDns`, `disconnected`, `blacklisted`, `warmup` (pooled over the mailboxes that report it: `mailboxes`, `sent`, `spam`, `spamRate`, `interval`) and `notReported`.
- `linkedinSeats`: seats have no domain. Each has `name`, `linkedinUrl`, `status`, `connected` and `issues`. Disconnected seats first.
- `mailboxesWithoutDomain`: sender refs whose domain is not known.
- `totals`: `senders`, `domains`, `high`, `medium`, `low`, `unknown` and `linkedinSeats`.

How the risk is set:

| Risk | When |
| --- | --- |
| `high` | A mailbox is on a block list, SPF, DKIM or MX fails, or the pooled warmup spam rate is over the threshold. |
| `medium` | DMARC fails, or a mailbox is disconnected. |
| `low` | None of the above, and the vendor reported at least one check. |
| `unknown` | The vendor reported nothing for any mailbox of the domain. |

- **Needs:** `senders.list`, `senders.health`.
- **Costs:** one listing of senders, plus 1 request per sender.
- **Notes it can add:** a cap was reached.
- **Does not:** look up DNS itself. It reports what the vendor says about each mailbox. A check the vendor reports for no mailbox of a domain is named in `notReported`, and a domain with nothing reported is `unknown`, not `low`.

## `senderCapacity`

Whether the senders can send what the campaigns ask.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `days` | 7 | 1 to 90 | How many full days before today to average the sends over. |
| `max_campaigns` | 25 | 1 to 200 | The most active campaigns to read. |
| `max_senders` | 200 | 1 to 1,000 | The most senders to read for the workspace, and for each campaign. |

`data` (`SenderCapacity`):

- `finding`: the answer in a sentence or three.
- `capacity`: `daily` (the daily limits of active senders added up), `mailboxDaily`, `linkedinDaily`, `activeSenders`, `withLimit`, `withoutLimit` and `inactiveSenders`.
- `ask`: `daily` (the daily limits of active campaigns added up), `campaigns`, `withLimit` and `withoutLimit`. `null` when the adapter cannot read campaign settings.
- `sends`: `averageDaily`, `total`, `days`, `daysWithData`, `from`, `to` and `scope` (`workspace` or `campaigns`). `null` when the adapter has no daily stats.
- `utilization`: average daily sends divided by daily capacity.
- `askRatio`: the daily ask divided by daily capacity. Over 1 means the campaigns ask for more than the senders can send.
- `shortCampaigns`: campaigns whose own senders cannot cover their daily limit, with `dailyLimit`, `senderCapacity`, `shortBy`, `senders`, `activeSenders` and a `finding`. Largest gap first. `null` when it could not be checked.
- `uncheckedCampaigns`: campaigns left out of `shortCampaigns` because a limit is not reported.

- **Needs:** `senders.list`. It reads more when the adapter has `campaigns.settings`, `senders.byCampaign` and `stats.byDay`.
- **Costs:** one listing of senders and one of active campaigns, then up to 2 requests per campaign and 1 for daily stats. Without workspace stats it reads daily stats per campaign: up to 3 per campaign.
- **Notes it can add:** the daily ask and the short campaigns were left out because the adapter cannot read campaign settings; the short campaigns were left out because it cannot list a campaign's senders; average daily sends were left out, or cover only the active campaigns read.
- **Does not:** count a sender or campaign with no reported limit as 0. They are counted apart, in `withoutLimit`. In `shortCampaigns`, a sender shared with another campaign is counted in full for each, so a campaign can look covered while its senders are oversubscribed: `askRatio` shows that. Only active campaigns are read.

## `bounceWatch`

The campaigns whose bounce rate (bounced divided by sent) is over a threshold, and the senders behind them.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `threshold` | 0.03 | 0 to 1 | The bounce rate that is too high. |
| `min_sent` | 100 | 0 or more, whole | A campaign with fewer sends than this is too small to judge. |
| `from` | none | `YYYY-MM-DD` | First day to count. Leave out for the vendor's whole history. |
| `to` | none | `YYYY-MM-DD` | Last day to count. Leave out for the vendor's whole history. |
| `include_senders` | `true` | boolean | Also check the senders of each campaign over the threshold. |
| `max_campaigns` | 50 | 1 to 500 | The most campaigns to check. |
| `max_senders_per_campaign` | 10 | 1 to 200 | The most senders to check for one campaign over the threshold. |

`data` (`BounceWatch`):

- `over`: campaigns over the threshold, worst first, each with `bounced`, `sent`, `rate`, `interval`, `status`, a `finding`, and `senders`: its senders that are themselves over the threshold (`null` when senders were not checked).
- `tooFewSends`: campaigns with fewer sends than `min_sent`, whatever their rate.
- `unknown`: campaigns the vendor reports no sent or bounce count for, with the `reason`.
- `totals`: `campaigns`, `over`, `clean`, `tooFewSends`, `unknown`, and `bounced`, `sent` and `rate` over the campaigns with known counts.
- `threshold`, `minSent`: what was used.

- **Needs:** `stats.campaign`. Checking senders also needs `stats.bySender` and `senders.byCampaign`.
- **Costs:** one listing of campaigns and 1 request per campaign, plus 1 listing and up to 1 request per sender for each campaign over the threshold.
- **Notes it can add:** senders were not checked because the adapter needs stats per sender and the list of a campaign's senders; a cap was reached.
- **Does not:** judge a sender within one campaign. A sender's rate is over everything it sent in the period, because no vendor reports one sender within one campaign. Every campaign is read, whatever its status. A campaign in `tooFewSends` or `unknown` is not clean.

## `warmupReadiness`

Which senders are not ready to send, each with its reasons.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `min_score` | 90 | 0 to 100 | The warmup score a mailbox needs to be ready. |
| `check_health` | `false` | boolean | Also check each mailbox's DNS records and warmup spam. |
| `max_spam_rate` | 0.1 | 0 to 1 | With `check_health`, a mailbox whose warmup emails land in spam more often than this share is not ready. |
| `max_senders` | 100 | 1 to 1,000 | The most senders to check. |

`data` (`WarmupReadiness`):

- `notReady`: the senders with at least one reason, most reasons first. Each has `kind`, `address`, `name`, `status`, `warmupEnabled`, `score`, `dailyLimit`, `reasons` and `findings` (each reason in plain words).
- `byReason`: how many senders have each reason.
- `ready`, `total`, `linkedinSeats`.
- `healthChecked`: `true` when DNS records and warmup spam were checked too.

The reasons:

| Reason | When |
| --- | --- |
| `not_active` | The sender's status is not `active`. |
| `warmup_off` | The vendor says warmup is off. |
| `score_low` | The warmup score is under `min_score`. |
| `score_unknown` | The vendor reports no warmup score. |
| `no_daily_limit` | No daily limit is set or reported. |
| `dns_failing` | With `check_health`: a DNS record fails. |
| `warmup_spam_high` | With `check_health`: warmup spam is over `max_spam_rate`. |

- **Needs:** `senders.list`. `check_health` also needs `senders.health`.
- **Costs:** one listing of senders, plus 1 request per mailbox with `check_health`.
- **Notes it can add:** DNS records and warmup spam were not checked because the adapter cannot read a sender's health; a cap was reached.
- **Does not:** treat a missing score as 0. A `null` score means the vendor does not report one, which counts as not ready under its own reason. A LinkedIn seat is judged on its status only.

## `senderLoad`

Senders that are overused or unused, and campaigns that depend on too few senders.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `max_campaigns_per_sender` | 3 | 1 or more, whole | A sender attached to more campaigns than this is overused. |
| `statuses` | `["active"]` | at least one campaign status | Campaign statuses to look at. |
| `max_campaigns` | 25 | 1 to 200 | The most campaigns to read. |
| `max_senders` | 200 | 1 to 1,000 | The most senders to read for the workspace, and for each campaign. |

`data` (`SenderLoad`):

- `overused`: senders in more campaigns than allowed, most loaded first, with `campaigns`, `over`, `campaignRefs` and a `finding`.
- `unused`: active senders attached to none of the campaigns read.
- `campaigns`: campaigns with an issue (`no_sender`, `single_sender` or `inactive_sender`), with `senders`, `issues`, the `inactive` senders and a `finding`.
- `totals`: `senders`, `activeSenders`, `campaigns`, `overused`, `unused` and `campaignsAtRisk`.
- `maxCampaignsPerSender`: what was used.

- **Needs:** `senders.list`, `senders.byCampaign`.
- **Costs:** one listing of senders and one of campaigns, then 1 request per campaign.
- **Notes it can add:** a cap was reached.
- **Does not:** count campaigns outside `statuses`. A sender used only by a paused campaign shows as unused unless `paused` is included. It counts campaigns, not volume: use `senderCapacity` for that.

## `settingsAudit`

Risky campaign settings, per campaign and by severity.

| Input | Default | Range | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `statuses` | `["active"]` | at least one campaign status | Campaign statuses to audit. |
| `max_window_hours` | 12 | 1 to 24 | A sending window longer than this is flagged. |
| `min_window_hours` | 2 | 0 to 24 | A sending window shorter than this is flagged. |
| `min_gap_minutes` | 5 | 0 or more | A wait between two sends shorter than this is flagged. |
| `max_daily_limit_per_sender` | 50 | 1 or more | A campaign's daily limit should not exceed this many sends per attached sender. |
| `max_campaigns` | 25 | 1 to 200 | The most campaigns to audit. |
| `max_senders_per_campaign` | 100 | 1 to 1,000 | The most senders to count for one campaign. |

`data` (`SettingsAudit`):

- `campaigns`: the campaigns with at least one finding, most serious first. Each has `name`, `status` and `findings` (each with `check`, `severity`, a `finding` sentence and the `value` behind it).
- `byCheck`: how many campaigns each check flagged.
- `totals`: `campaigns`, `withFindings`, `clean`, and the number of `high`, `medium` and `low` findings.

The checks:

| Check | Severity | Fires when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `limit_over_senders` | high | The daily limit is over `max_daily_limit_per_sender` times the number of attached senders. |
| `end_date_passed` | high | The campaign is active but its end date has passed. |
| `reply_does_not_stop` | high | A lead who replies keeps getting the next steps. |
| `no_window` | medium | No sending window is set or reported. |
| `window_long` | medium | A window is longer than `max_window_hours`. |
| `no_daily_limit` | medium | No daily limit is set or reported. |
| `gap_short` | medium | Sends are closer together than `min_gap_minutes`. |
| `open_tracking` | medium | Open tracking is on. |
| `click_tracking` | medium | Click tracking is on. |
| `weekend` | low | A window includes Saturday or Sunday. |
| `window_short` | low | A window is shorter than `min_window_hours`. |
| `no_timezone` | low | A window has no timezone. |

- **Needs:** `campaigns.settings`. Comparing the daily limit with the sender count also needs `senders.byCampaign`.
- **Costs:** one listing of campaigns, then 1 request per campaign, or 2 when its senders can be listed.
- **Notes it can add:** the daily limit was not compared with the sender count because the adapter cannot list a campaign's senders; a cap was reached.
- **Does not:** tell a missing setting from one that is not set, for a window, a timezone or a daily limit: the vendor cannot tell them apart either, so all three are flagged. Any other setting the vendor reports nothing for is skipped. The severities are fixed.
