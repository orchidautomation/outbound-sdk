POST /campaign/GetCampaignsForLead
The campaigns one lead is in, each with the campaign's status and the lead's status in it. Name the lead by email, LinkedIn id or profile URL.
https://docs.heyreach.io/hey-reach-api/campaigns/get-campaigns-for-lead
  email (string, in body): The lead's email address.
  linkedinId (string, in body): The lead's LinkedIn id, `linkedin_id` in lead payloads.
  profileUrl (string, in body): The lead's LinkedIn profile URL.
  limit (number, in body): How many campaigns to return.
  offset (number, in body): How many campaigns to skip.
